# SmartOnboard Product Vision

## Product Overview

SmartOnboard is a Verified Hiring Ecosystem and AI Hiring Operating System.

The platform is designed to help recruiters discover, evaluate, and hire qualified candidates through trusted signals, verification, and AI-assisted decision making.

Unlike traditional job boards, SmartOnboard does not optimize for application volume.

It optimizes for hiring quality.

The platform exists to solve a fundamental hiring problem:

Recruiters are overwhelmed with low-quality applications, while qualified candidates struggle to find jobs that genuinely match their experience and skills.

SmartOnboard aims to create a trusted environment where both sides are verified and where AI assists rather than replaces human decision-making.

---

# Mission

Improve hiring quality through trust, verification, and intelligent matching.

---

# Vision

Become the most trusted hiring ecosystem for both recruiters and candidates.

A platform where:

* Recruiters trust the applications they receive.
* Candidates trust the opportunities they discover.
* AI improves hiring decisions without replacing human judgment.

---

# Core Philosophy

## Trust Over Volume

Most hiring platforms optimize for:

* More applications
* More clicks
* More engagement

SmartOnboard optimizes for:

* Better applicants
* Better matches
* Better hiring outcomes

Application quantity is not a success metric.

Hiring quality is.

---

## Verified Participants

Trust begins with verification.

### Candidates

Candidates should verify:

* Email address
* Phone number

Future roadmap:

* Identity verification
* Employment verification

---

### Recruiters

Recruiters should verify:

* Company email
* Company domain
* DNS records
* MX records

Future roadmap:

* Company verification
* Trusted employer status

---

# Closed Ecosystem

SmartOnboard is intentionally a closed ecosystem.

Unverified participants should not gain full platform access.

This creates a higher trust environment and reduces spam, fake jobs, and low-quality activity.

---

# Candidate Experience

Candidates should be able to:

* Create a profile
* Upload up to three resumes
* Discover relevant jobs
* View applicability percentages
* Understand why a job matches their background
* Apply with the most relevant resume
* Track applications
* Track interviews

Candidates should not need to manually search through hundreds of irrelevant opportunities.

---

# Applicability Percentage

Applicability Percentage is a core SmartOnboard feature.

Purpose:

Help candidates understand how well their experience aligns with a job before applying.

Candidates should be able to see:

* Applicability Percentage
* Matching Skills
* Missing Skills
* Best Matching Resume

Candidates should not need to open every job to understand relevance.

Applicability Percentage should be visible directly on job cards.

---

# Resume Philosophy

Candidates may store multiple resumes.

Examples:

* AI Engineer Resume
* Product Manager Resume
* Founder Resume

These resumes contribute to a unified skill graph.

The system should identify which resume best matches each opportunity.

During application:

The candidate selects a specific resume.

That resume becomes the immutable application snapshot.

Future edits must not affect historical applications.

---

# Recruiter Experience

Recruiters should be able to:

* Publish jobs
* Import jobs
* Review applications
* Schedule interviews
* Manage hiring decisions

Recruiters should spend less time filtering noise and more time evaluating strong candidates.

---

# AI Philosophy

AI is a copilot.

AI assists recruiters and candidates.

AI does not replace them.

AI may:

* Rank applicants
* Highlight risks
* Summarize candidates
* Recommend interview pools
* Explain applicability percentages

AI must never:

* Auto-hire
* Auto-reject
* Auto-schedule interviews
* Make final hiring decisions

Recruiters always retain final authority.

---

# Information Boundaries

Different users should see different information.

## Candidate Visibility

Allowed:

* Applicability Percentage
* Matching Skills
* Missing Skills
* Best Matching Resume

Restricted:

* Risk Scores
* Authenticity Scores
* Evidence Scores
* Recruiter Rankings
* Recruiter Notes

---

## Recruiter Visibility

Recruiters may view:

* Rankings
* Risk Scores
* Authenticity Scores
* Evidence Scores
* AI Hiring Briefs

These insights assist recruiter decision-making.

---

# Fairness Model

To reduce first-applicant bias:

AI ranking should evaluate candidate pools after the review window closes.

Candidates should be assessed against the complete pool rather than application order.

The system should prioritize fairness and consistency.

---

# Monetization

Candidates are free.

Recruiters pay:

$49 per month per active job.

Rules:

* Open jobs are billed.
* Closed jobs are not billed.
* No feature paywalls.
* No AI paywalls.

Every recruiter receives the same core product.

---

# Product Boundary

Offer Accepted.

It remains in the repository but is not part of the current hiring-focused roadmap.
