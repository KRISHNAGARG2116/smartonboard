# SmartOnboard AI Architecture

## AI Philosophy

AI is a copilot.

AI assists decision making.

AI never makes final hiring decisions.

---

## Candidate AI Features

### Applicability Engine

Outputs:

* Applicability Percentage
* Matching Skills
* Missing Skills
* Best Matching Resume

---

## Recruiter AI Features

### Evidence Score

Measures:

* Proof of Work
* Project Evidence
* Experience Validation

---

### Authenticity Score

Measures:

* Keyword Stuffing
* JD Mirroring
* Resume Manipulation

---

### Risk Score

Measures:

* Timeline Gaps
* Data Inconsistencies
* Credential Concerns

---

## Hiring Brief

Provides:

* Match Counts
* High Risk Counts
* Tailored Resume Counts
* Interview Recommendations

---

## Ranking Pipeline

Applications

↓

Vector Similarity

↓

Top Candidates

↓

AI Analysis

↓

Ranking

↓

Recruiter Review

---

## Security Rule

Candidates must never receive:

* Risk Scores
* Authenticity Scores
* Evidence Scores
* Internal Rankings
