# Steep Gap Analysis V2 — Visual Perception Report

This report evaluates **SmartOnboard** screens compared to **Steep.app** visual presentation, detailing the visual perception gaps and identifying what is missing visually, compositionally, emotionally, structurally, and why Steep feels more premium.

---

## 1. Landing Page

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   Steep's landing page behaves like a high-end digital editorial magazine. It frames the product value using monumental display serif headings paired with clean, lightweight outline illustrations and floating product cards that orbit the header. Spacing is comfortable, section transitions feel soft, and the CTA group is compact and focused.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   A polished layout pairing display serifs with high-fidelity, centered workspace previews. The page lacks high-contrast visual anchors to draw the visitor's eye.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Rhythm and whitespace variance. The spacing between sections is uniform, making the page feel like a boilerplate template rather than a custom-tailored product narrative.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   Immediate authority. A first-time visitor is presented with a standard SaaS feature grid rather than an interactive hiring story that builds trust.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   A clear candidate-to-recruiter workflow loop. The features are presented as disjointed blocks rather than a single, secure sourcing journey.

---

## 2. Recruiter Dashboard (`/recruiter/dashboard`)

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   Steep's dashboard feels like a physical desk with elevated ceramic cards on a soft fog-grey canvas. HAirlines are thin and crisp, status badges are clean, and typography weights (rather than heavy colors) establish structural hierarchy.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   Usability and contrast. Selected applicants in the queue list have black text on black backgrounds, making candidate names invisible in light mode. Text inside skill chips and the AI Copilot Hub header are white-on-fog, making them unreadable.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Density. The card borders and heights are loose, creating too much vertical dead space. Spacing does not cluster related metrics together.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   Professional reliability. The dashboard feels broken and unpolished when key text, inputs, and selection states disappear into their backgrounds.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   A legible form design. Modals have inputs with white borders and white text on a white card background, rendering form fields invisible.

---

## 3. Candidate Dashboard (`/candidate/dashboard`)

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   Steep uses generous rounded corners (`24px`), custom checklist tiles, and pill actions that feel friendly and tangible.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   Primary CTA visibility. The "Resolve" checklist buttons and profile action links are black-on-black, looking like empty dark blocks.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Rhythm. Sidebar widgets like completion graphs, verification badges, and biographies are unevenly spaced, resulting in misaligned columns.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   A sense of verified confidence. Candidates want to feel verified and matches-ready, but are blocked by illegible button states and form overrides.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   A polished checklist layout. Checklist items look like form fields rather than integrated status tiles.

---

## 4. Jobs Board (`/recruiter/jobs`)

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   Jobs and postings tables use extremely fine Sohne weights (e.g. 450) and compact vertical padding to present dense, structured lists that look clean and intentional.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   A high-contrast header and list items. Table headers are heavily bolded, creating visual noise, while the cells use loose padding that wastes screen real estate.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Cell height rhythm. The spacing between columns is uniform and lacks secondary text contrast.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   The feel of a professional database. It feels like a standard template rather than a premium, daylight admin control panel.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   Compact column groupings. Labels, status tags, and candidate counts are spread out too loosely.

---

## 5. Applications List (`/recruiter/candidates`)

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   It uses clean outline badges, soft rows, and subtle hover lifts to navigate candidates smoothly without heavy border indicators.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   Contrast and high-contrast row selections. Unselected rows use heavy grey dividers, while selected rows turn solid black, causing text to vanish.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Grouped candidate metadata. Status badges, fit percentages, and application dates have the same layout prominence.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   Confidence in hiring quality. The candidate pool list feels hard to parse and visually tiring.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   A clean row-hover indicator that highlights candidates subtly without shifting layouts.

---

## 6. Pipeline Board (`/recruiter/pipeline`)

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   Pipeline columns are styled with solid hairline borders and elevated cards that float on drag with a soft shadow offset, making the kanban board feel physical and premium.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   A clean drop target highlight and solid card borders. Columns have dashed borders that feel like placeholder cards rather than solid slots.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Compact column spacing. Wide columns force unnecessary horizontal scroll bars.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   A satisfying workflow transition. Dragging candidates does not feel tactile or physical.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   Micro-elevation indicators when candidates are being hovered or dragged.

---

## 7. Analytics Dashboard (`/recruiter/analytics`)

1. **Why does Steep feel more premium?**
   Analytics visualizations use exactly two chromatic voices (warm rust and cool blue) to show trends, leaving the rest of the layout achromatic and clean.
2. **What is visually missing?**
   Integrated chart tooltips and clean borders. Chart tooltips use heavy drop-shadows and dark backgrounds that break the daylight design language.
3. **What is compositionally missing?**
   Metric and graph alignment. Graphs take up too much vertical height, pushing secondary metric summaries off-screen.
4. **What is emotionally missing?**
   The feel of a curated financial/analytics dashboard.
5. **What is structurally missing?**
   A clean 3-column layout that groups key metrics alongside trend charts.
